# Hidden Hunter assets

Warehouse environment art (floor, walls, barrels, crates, machines, labels)
is original procedural canvas drawing created for this project.

Character sprites are third-party CC0 assets copied into this repo. They are
not hotlinked at runtime. The monster is original art drawn for this game.

No League of Legends, Riot, Dead by Daylight, Resident Evil, or other commercial-game assets are used.

The monster is original because the CC0 candidates did not fit this camera. The 0x72 dungeon demon and the OpenGameArt “Animated Top Down Zombie” skeleton both read as side or three-quarter views. Kenney’s own zombie is top-down, but it is an armed cartoon person and reads as another player. The creature here is drawn from above, in the same small flat style as the Kenney hunter and tracker, with a long-armed silhouette and red eyes.
