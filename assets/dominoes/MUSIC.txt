Dominoes background music
==========================

The generated calm-loop.wav track has been removed.

Dominoes plays the files named in playlist.json, in that order, from this folder.
Each entry is a filename only, for example "song.mp3".
When one track ends, the next one starts. After the last track, the first starts again.
The Mute button on the Dominoes screen pauses and resumes this playlist only.
Volume stays low so it does not cover the game sound effects.

playlist.json is empty until the three Dominoes tracks are in this folder and listed here.
