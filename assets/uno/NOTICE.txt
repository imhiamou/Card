UNO presentation assets
=========================

Card artwork
------------
Source: 4 Colour Cards by VerzatileDev
Page: https://verzatiledev.itch.io/4colour
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Crediting the author is not required.
Files: assets/uno/cards/*.png
Use: number cards 0–9, skip, reverse, and draw two in red, blue, green, and yellow;
     wild, wild draw four, and the card back.
The repository stores 200px slices of the pack's numbered and special spritesheets.
The original sheets and example project are not included.

Inspected and not shipped
--------------------------
Kenney Playing Cards Pack (CC0), https://kenney.nl/assets/playing-cards-pack
and https://opengameart.org/content/playing-cards-pack
The pack is a standard four-suit deck. It was not mixed with the UNO cards.

Naro Game "UNO Game Assets" (https://narogame.itch.io/uno-game-assets) was not
downloaded. The primary pack already includes every card type this game uses,
so a second deck was not added.
